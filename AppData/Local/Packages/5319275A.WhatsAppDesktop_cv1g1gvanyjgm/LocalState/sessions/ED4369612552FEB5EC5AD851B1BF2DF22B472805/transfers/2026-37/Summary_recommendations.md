[Key Data Findings
The Silver Tier Anomaly: Interestingly, Silver members yield the highest average purchase amount ($203.40), outperforming both Gold ($189.68) and Bronze ($177.69).

Electronics Drive High-Value Sales: The "Electronics" category generates the highest average order value, specifically among older demographics. Customers aged 60+ average $456.96 per electronics transaction, and those aged 46-60 average $438.34.

Weak Age and Tenure Correlations: There is almost no mathematical correlation between a customer's age and their purchase amount (0.06), or their tenure and purchase amount (0.04). High spending is driven by the product category rather than loyalty tenure.

Strategic Recommendations
Audit the Gold Tier Program: Investigate why Gold members are spending less per transaction than Silver members. The current Gold benefits might be encouraging frequent, low-value purchases rather than higher overall spending.

Target Older Demographics with High-Ticket Items: Reallocate advertising spend for premium electronics toward the 46+ age brackets, as they are mathematically your most lucrative segment for this category.

Revamp Retention Incentives: Since simply being a long-term customer (tenure) doesn't organically translate to higher spending, introduce milestone-based discounts that specifically require a minimum cart value to redeem.](ecommerce_customers.csv)
